Monetization guide

Four proven ways a website can earn. Tap each one to expand.

Affiliate links

Recommend products you actually use and earn a commission when readers buy. Start with one or two programs (Amazon Associates, or the affiliate page of your favorite software). Be open about the link, and write honest reviews so readers trust you.

Display ads

Ad networks pay per thousand views. Income is small until you have steady traffic, so treat ads as a later layer. Most networks want a few months of original content and a few thousand monthly visits first.

Digital products

Templates, planners, guides and presets cost nothing to ship and keep every sale. Start with a free sample (like the ones above) to build an email list, then offer a paid version.

Tips and memberships

A tip jar lets readers say thanks with no commitment. Memberships go further: a small monthly fee for extra content or early access. Both work best when you already have a loyal audience.
